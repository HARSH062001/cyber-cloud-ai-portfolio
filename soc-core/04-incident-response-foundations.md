# 04 Incident Response Foundations

Incident response is the structured process used to prepare for, detect, contain, remove, and recover from security incidents.

## Preparation

Preparation includes creating procedures, defining roles, training staff, improving visibility, and making sure evidence sources are available.

## Identification

Identification is the stage where suspicious behaviour is recognised as a possible or confirmed incident.

## Containment

Containment focuses on limiting damage. This may include isolating hosts, disabling accounts, blocking indicators, or restricting access.

## Eradication

Eradication is the removal of the cause of the incident, such as malware, persistence mechanisms, malicious accounts, or risky configurations.

## Recovery

Recovery is the process of restoring systems and operations safely while continuing to monitor for recurrence.

## Lessons Learned

Lessons learned help improve detection, response processes, documentation, and control gaps after an incident is handled.

SOC analysts often contribute to the identification, triage, documentation, and early containment stages. Even at entry level, understanding the full lifecycle helps analysts make better escalation and reporting decisions.
