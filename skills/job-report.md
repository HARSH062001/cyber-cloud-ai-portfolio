# Job Report

## Purpose

Track job applications, follow-ups, interview stages, and career progress in a structured format.

## When to Use

Use when the user asks to log a job application, update a hiring stage, prepare a follow-up summary, or organize job-search activity.

## Inputs Required

- Company name
- Role title
- Application date
- Link or source
- Current status
- Notes or next step

## Output Format

- Company
- Role
- Source or link
- Status
- Next step
- Notes
- Short job update summary

## Notion Database

`Job Tracker`

## GitHub Impact

None by default.

## Discord Channel

`#dept-chro`

## Human Approval Required

Yes. Job workflows can affect real applications and communication.

## Safety Notes

Never apply to jobs, send outreach, or submit forms without approval. Keep summaries factual and avoid inventing interview progress.
