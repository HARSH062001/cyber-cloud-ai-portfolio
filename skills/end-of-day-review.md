# End-of-Day Review

## Purpose

Close the workday with a compact review of completed tasks, blockers, energy, and tomorrow's first actions.

## When to Use

Use at the end of the day, after a study block, or when the user wants a reset before tomorrow.

## Inputs Required

- Completed tasks
- Wins
- Blockers
- Energy or burnout signal
- Tomorrow priorities

## Output Format

- What got done
- Wins
- Blockers
- Energy note
- Tomorrow top priorities
- Short closing summary

## Notion Database

`Daily Journal`

## GitHub Impact

None by default.

## Discord Channel

`#jim-office`

## Human Approval Required

No. Normal private review is low risk.

## Safety Notes

Keep it honest and concise. If burnout or overload is visible, recommend lighter planning instead of adding more tasks.
