# Startup Research Brief

## Purpose

Capture startup ideas in a controlled format without letting research distract from core study and career priorities.

## When to Use

Use when the user wants to save an idea, compare startup concepts, or preserve a market thought for later review.

## Inputs Required

- Idea name
- Problem
- Customer
- Market notes
- Current status
- Why it matters later

## Output Format

- Idea name
- Problem statement
- Target customer
- Status
- Research notes
- Future opportunity summary

## Notion Database

`Startup Research`

## GitHub Impact

None by default.

## Discord Channel

`#dept-cso`

## Human Approval Required

No for logging research notes. Yes if the work expands into public content or a live business action.

## Safety Notes

This skill is lower priority than PTE, cyber study, and career tasks. Do not let startup research take over the day before core work is complete.
